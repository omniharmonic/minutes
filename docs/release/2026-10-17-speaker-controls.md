# Speaker controls — build 50

Live captions now keep anonymous, provisional speaker groups independently of remembered identity. Usable single-speaker evidence produces a bounded session-local Speaker 1 / Speaker 2 label; repeated evidence reuses a group only with cosine similarity >=0.90 and a >=0.12 margin. Ambiguity creates a separate provisional group rather than merging voices. Mixed, inconsistent or insufficient evidence remains unresolved. This is conservative window-level grouping, not word-level diarization of overlapping speech; speakers can be over-segmented.

Speaker labels are clickable. A popover offers a typed name, calendar invitees and other saved people. A manual name updates every retained caption in that provisional group and future windows matched to it. The control explicitly states that these are live/session-only labels: they do not enroll voiceprints or change the final transcript. The saved recording uses independent full-recording diarization and requires review. Remembered matches retain a “likely” qualifier.

Saved-meeting reconciliation separates Calendar invitees from Other people, retains name/email search, and offers Add person not on the invitation. Manual additions do not appear as calendar invitees. Existing confirmation saves the person/cluster/transcript assignment; remembering a voice remains a separate explicit option. Invitees are candidates, not evidence that someone attended or spoke.

Validation: 353 model-free tests passed and iOS simulator build succeeded. Mac package signed and notarized. Installed native 60-second silent preview produced distinct Speaker 1 and Speaker 2 controls; manually naming Speaker 1 updated an earlier and a later matching caption while Speaker 2 stayed separate. Final diagnostics: six sessions, zero retries, zero dropped batches. No microphone, playback, person creation or enrollment occurred. Condition testing is documented in the voice-reuse report and counts-only audit JSON; noise and short band-limited matching still fail the broad reliability gate.
