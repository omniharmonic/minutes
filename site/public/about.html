Your meetings.
Your device.

Minutes records meetings, transcribes audio on device, separates speakers, and helps you turn conversations into useful notes. A native app for Mac and iPhone, published by Benjamin Gleason Ross / OmniHarmonic.

Download for Mac

Start simply

Install the local speaker models in Settings, approve microphone and Speech Recognition access, then choose Record. On Mac, you can also capture device audio with the appropriate system permission. iPhone records through its microphone; it does not promise audio capture from other apps or phone calls.

Recognize familiar voices

Listen to a speaker sample, confirm their name, and optionally remember that voice with their consent. Strong matches can prefill names in later meetings, and you can correct them. Voice profiles currently stay on the device where you saved them.

Connect your calendar

Google Calendar access is read-only. Minutes reads your calendar list and upcoming events, including titles, call links and invitees, to connect a recording to the right meeting. It never creates or edits your Google events. Invitees and RSVP responses are context, not proof of who attended or spoke.

Mark what matters

Tap the bookmark while you record to mark a moment. Moments sit on the recording's timeline and beside the transcript, and every point in the summary links back to the moment it came from.

Your Mac and iPhone, in step

Optional iCloud sync keeps meetings, transcripts, notes and moments the same on your devices, using your own private iCloud storage. Remembered voices stay on the device that learned them.

Export when you choose

Keep notes on your device, share or save Markdown, or sign in to your own Parachute vault and pick the folder where meetings are saved. Exports go only where you send them.

Transcripts, speaker names and summaries are generated automatically and can be wrong; review them. Let everyone know when you record: consent laws vary.
