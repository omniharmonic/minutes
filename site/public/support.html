Minutes support.

Before your first recording

Open Settings → On-device speech and install the speaker models. Approve Microphone and Speech Recognition when requested. On Mac, system audio also needs Screen & System Audio Recording permission. Use a short non-private test recording before an important meeting.

Calendar connection

Choose Google Calendar → Connect Google and approve the two read-only Calendar permissions. The app cannot edit your calendar. If Google's verification or testing restrictions block your account, check the release notes and contact support. Apple Calendar is an alternative on supported devices.

Speaker names and samples

Open People within a meeting to listen to a sample and confirm who spoke. Remember voice is optional and needs at least 20 seconds of compatible usable speech. Old-model profiles may need a new sample. A saved embedding alone cannot reconstruct audio: listening needs the original timed recording. Mac and iPhone profiles are currently separate.

Audio route changes

iPhone prefers its built-in microphone. If an audio device connects or disappears, Minutes attempts to recover capture while preserving saved audio; a brief gap can remain. Check the visible recording source and save a short test. If capture fails, original saved audio is retained where available for recovery.

Get help

Report a reproducible issue or email synergy@benjaminlife.one. Include app version, device/OS, steps and sanitized error wording. Never post recordings, transcripts, invitation links, passwords, codes, tokens or voice-profile data.
