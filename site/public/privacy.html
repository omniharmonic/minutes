Privacy, plainly.

Effective October 17, 2026 · Minutes by Benjamin Gleason Ross / OmniHarmonic

Recording and on-device processing

Minutes starts recording only after your explicit action. The app stores audio, transcripts, notes, speaker assignments and remembered voice profiles locally. Speech recognition is required to run on device; unsupported configurations show an error instead of silently uploading meeting audio for cloud recognition. Installing speaker models downloads them from the model provider.

Optional Apple Intelligence summaries use Apple's on-device Foundation Models where supported. Minutes has no advertising or analytics SDK and does not send meeting content to an AI training service. Generated summaries and speaker identities may be inaccurate and should be reviewed.

Google Calendar data

If you connect Google, Minutes requests permission to read your subscribed calendar list and calendar events. It uses calendar names and identifiers to select calendars, and meeting titles, times, conference links, attendee names, email addresses and RSVP statuses to show upcoming meetings, offer reminders and associate a recording with the selected event.

Calendar access and refresh tokens are stored in your device's Keychain. Minutes does not create, modify or delete Google events. Connecting Google does not consent to recording a meeting or saving anyone's voice. Google's authentication and Calendar services receive the requests necessary for sign-in and calendar access.

Minutes follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used to provide the meeting features described here, not for advertising, sale, unrelated profiling, or training generalized AI/ML models.

Voice profiles

Remembering a voice is a separate optional action. Only save a person's voice with their consent. Voice profiles help suggest identities in later meetings and currently remain on the enrolling device. You can forget them from the People or Settings interface. Ordinary meeting exports do not include biometric vectors or raw audio.

Optional exports and servers

If you explicitly export notes or connect Prism, selected meeting text and participant context are sent to the destination and vault you configure. That destination has its own administrators, permissions, privacy practices and retention controls. A configured server may be self-hosted; it is not required for local recording or transcription. Exported files and remote copies must be deleted separately.

Retention and controls

Local meeting data remains until you remove it. There is no promised automatic deletion schedule. Use Disconnect in Calendar settings to remove local Google credentials and request token revocation, or revoke Minutes in your Google Account connections. Disconnecting does not erase already saved meeting records or exports. You may delete local recordings and forget saved voices independently. Device backups and exports may retain separate copies.

Website and testing

This website has no analytics scripts, advertising cookies or sign-in forms. The HTTPS hosting provider may process ordinary network metadata to deliver pages. If you test via TestFlight, Apple may collect and share beta diagnostics and feedback under its own privacy practices. If you submit an issue or email, that service receives the information you choose to send; avoid including private recordings, transcripts, tokens or voice data.
